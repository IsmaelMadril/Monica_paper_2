Each characteristic partitions its stated cohort; do not add counts across characteristic blocks. Unknown ethnicity (source 9) is retained separately from Other; true nulls, if present, appear as Missing. A zero stay category is shown explicitly even when empty; 15–30 and 31+ are mutually exclusive. Residence comparisons exclude all 2015–2017 records because their area labels remain provisional; all other blocks retain them. The missingness and scope files give exact exclusion/outcome denominators.

One exploratory omnibus comparison per characteristic: uncorrected Pearson chi-square only when every observed and expected cell is ≥5; sparse 2×2 tables use two-sided Fisher exact; larger sparse tables use fixed-margin Monte Carlo Pearson tests with 99,999 replicates and deterministic per-comparison seeds. Zero-margin rows/columns are not tested. Monte Carlo p=(exceedances+1)/(replicates+1), including ties. Simulation errors and tail-probability intervals are in provenance/t06/independence_tests.csv. Full numerical p-values are retained there. Display precision is three significant digits; no multiplicity adjustment or significance highlighting.

Tests assume independent admissions; repeated persons/facilities are unresolved. Small p-values do not measure clinical importance, causal effects, population cancer risks or temporal trends. Stay is observed during admission and is descriptive here. Column composition percentages are not within-category fatality or individual risk.
